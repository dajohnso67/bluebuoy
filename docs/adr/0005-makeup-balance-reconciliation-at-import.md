# Make-up balances at import: replay the rows, reconcile to the counter

Status: accepted (2026-09-16 · wayfinder ticket [#16](https://github.com/dajohnso67/bluebuoy/issues/16))

FileMaker shows staff six make-up counters per student (`MU_SP_Total`, `MU_PR_Total`, `MU_PM_Total`, `MU_GR_Total`, `MU_ST_Total`, `MU_Unknown_Total`). The DDR shows each is a stored number computed as flagged out-lessons minus flagged make-up schedules for that denomination and re-evaluated only when `Students.flag_update` fires; scripts also write the counters directly, and sibling transfers and goodwill credits have been applied to them by hand with a note. The [data profile of the 2026-09-14 export](../discovery/data-profile-2026-09-14.md) rehearsed [ADR-0003](./0003-denominated-makeup-credit-ledger.md)'s replay and found that FileMaker's own formula reproduces the stored value for only 14–31% of students with activity per denomination, and the ADR-0003 rule for 22–42%; fourteen counters are negative and 651 students carry an "Unknown" total that is simply minus their count of adult-type redemptions. The counters are stale, and the staleness cannot be separated mechanically from the deliberate hand edits the rows never captured. The Scheduling & search gate as first written — match per student, or classify every difference — would have put two to three thousand students on a classification list.

We decided that **the ledger is replayed from the rows under the ADR-0003 rule, and then reconciled to the stored counter by one event per student per denomination.** Every `Lesson_Out` row becomes an issued credit (do-not-issue rows issued-then-voided), every make-up on `Lesson_Schedules` a consumption of the amount recorded in the denomination `Lesson_MU_to_use` names, and then a `credit_event` of kind `reconciliation` — recording the stored counter, the replayed balance, and their difference, authored by the import — issues or consumes exactly that difference, so the go-live balance equals the number staff quoted the family last week. The reconciliation is reconciled to the ADR-0003 replay, not to FileMaker's counting, so the row-level history reads as business truth (a void is a void, a private make-up paid with two semi-privates consumed two) and FileMaker's counting quirks are absorbed into the reconciliation event together with the staleness. A negative counter imports as zero, with the negative recorded on the event and a mandatory review flag, because a family cannot owe make-ups. The Unknown denomination is not imported at all; its 651 students join the adult review list that [ADR-0004](./0004-adult-class-packs.md) already creates, because the number is an adult-redemption count wearing a credit's clothing.

The office reviews every student with an open enrollment or a lesson in the last twelve months whose reconciliation is non-zero — the population that will ask about a balance this year — and confirms or escalates; management corrects, through a reversal and a corrected event, per ADR-0003's management-only reversal. Confirmation is recorded on the event. Every other reconciliation imports marked unreviewed; when such a family books a make-up the marker shows on the booking screen and the office reviews it then, with booking never blocked. The **Scheduling & search gate** becomes: every reconciliation in the review population is confirmed or corrected before go-live; the count of unreviewed reconciliations outside it is reported; and the total reconciliation liability, valued in private-lesson equivalents, is reported to ownership and accepted. No family's balance changes at go-live, so batch 2 asks staff only two things: a heads-up on the review list's size, and whether to accept the unreviewed remainder wholesale or leave it flagged until each family returns.

Considered and rejected: the stored counter as the sole opening balance (imports thousands of provably stale numbers as if audited, with no provenance for any of them); the replayed ledger as the sole truth (auditable, but several thousand balances change at go-live in both directions and every hand-noted transfer is lost); an opening `credit` of kind `makeup` in place of a reconciliation event (a reconciliation is not a missed lesson, and the reviewer needs both reconciled numbers on the event); blocking a booking until an unreviewed reconciliation is confirmed (recreates the gate-the-roll problem ADR-0001 forbids).

The reconciliation runs against the cutover export, not the September one; the September numbers are the rehearsal that sized the review list. The import is repeatable (rule 8): rerunning it replaces every reconciliation event that no one has confirmed or corrected and leaves the confirmed ones untouched.
