# One denominated make-up credit ledger, imported by replay

Status: accepted (2026-09-10 · wayfinder ticket [#11](https://github.com/dajohnso67/bluebuoy/issues/11))

FileMaker shows six make-up counters per student (`MU_PR_Total`, `MU_SP_Total`, `MU_GR_Total`, `MU_PM_Total`, `MU_ST_Total`, plus a dead Adult one), which read as six independent balances. Staff confirmed in Round 2 (qa.md Q100) that credits convert between types at fixed rates in every direction — two semi-private make one private, two group make one semi-private, four group make one private, with Group, Stroke Tech and Parent & Me one "group" unit — that staff choose the conversion when the make-up is booked, and that credits move between siblings by hand. The DDR then showed the counters were never balances: each is an auto-enter calculation, out-lessons issued minus scheduled make-ups redeemed, and the redemption row already records the type being spent (`Lesson_MU_to_use`) and how many (`Lesson_MU_Amount`). The legacy system is an event ledger with derived balances, and the "six balances" reading was a misreading.

We decided the ledger is **one currency in three denominations** — private, semi-private, group — counted in whole lessons, with the missed lesson's type kept only as provenance. Conversion consumes exactly the table quantity and issues exactly the table quantity (leftovers stay put), happens inside redemption at staff's choice with a standalone action reserved to management, and is a row per direction in `credit_conversion_rate` so a future asymmetry is data. Transfers are household-only, any denomination, staff-initiated, and keep the credit's origin and expiry. Booking a make-up **reserves** the credit; the lesson occurring **consumes** it; a school-cancelled make-up **releases** it, in whatever denomination it was converted to. Expiry is a rule row (`makeup_expiry_months`, null meaning never — qa.md Q47 is still open) stamped onto `valid_to` at issue; converted credits inherit the earliest source expiry, reserved credits are protected from the nightly sweep, and management may extend. Liability reports in private-lesson equivalents, dollars only as a column computed at read. The ledger is append-only: a reversal is a new event citing the one it undoes, management only. Eligibility on the slot governs what a group credit can be spent on; the credit itself is fungible. Adult absences issue no credit — the class-pack model covers attendance.

The import **replays** history rather than copying balances: every `Lesson_Out` row becomes an issued credit (rows flagged do-not-issue import as issued-then-voided, void dated at import), every make-up on `Lesson_Schedules` a consumption in the denomination it names, and the replayed balance is compared per student to the stored counter, with every difference a reconciliation exception. Two known causes are waiting there: an attendance script edits the counters directly, and the do-not-issue flag never suppressed the credit flag, so a no-credit absence still counted as a credit.

Considered and rejected: six separate balances (the business converts between them daily, so liability and eligibility for a make-up would both be wrong); importing the six stored counters as opening balances (discards dates the expiry rule needs, and inherits the two defects silently); consuming the credit at booking as FileMaker does (a make-up of a make-up would then be a re-issue, and the offer queue already needs a held state).

One thing this surfaced that nobody had mentioned: the FileMaker redemption script refuses a make-up on any lesson ending after December 25. It is carried as a rule row (`makeup_redeem_before`, seeded to match legacy) until staff say whether it is policy or an artifact of the annual rollover (qa.md Q109).
