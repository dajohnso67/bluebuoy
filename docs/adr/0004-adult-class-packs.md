# Class packs: a second billing mode beside monthly tuition

Status: accepted (2026-09-10 · wayfinder ticket [#12](https://github.com/dajohnso67/bluebuoy/issues/12))

Staff confirmed in Round 2 (qa.md Q61) that the Adult class does not use monthly tuition: adults buy a single class or a pack of four or eight, and a lesson is deducted only when the teacher's roll marks them present. Unannounced no-shows lose the seat and deduct nothing, which staff want addressed. The DDR shows the legacy file has no support for any of this — the only artifact is `Lesson_Schedules.Adult_Credit`, a plain number hand-typed on the iPad that no script reads or maintains — and that FileMaker in fact prices Adult as a monthly tier (`Billing_Rates.Rate_Tier_AD` feeding `Monthly_Fee`), so adult students carry ordinary billing-month rows and appear on Payments Due. The pack model lives in staff's heads and on that one field.

We decided that **class packs are a general billing mode on the enrollment**, not an Adult special case: `enrollment.billing_mode` is `monthly` or `class_pack`, a rule row names which class types sell packs (seeded: Adult), and the billing run skips pack-mode enrollments entirely. A pack is a purchase, not a credit — a separate `class_pack` table with the same append-only discipline as the make-up ledger (ADR-0003), never a fourth denomination, because packs answer "what was paid for" and credits "what is owed back", and they never convert into each other. A sale is an `invoice` of kind `class_pack` with one line at the snapshot per-pack price (a `price` row with cadence `per_pack` and a quantity), paid at once, so revenue and refunds stay in the one money ledger. `attendance.marked` present consumes one lesson through a `class_pack_use` row that references the attendance row uniquely, so an offline replay can never consume twice. The instructor is never gated at the roll: an adult with no remaining lessons is still marked present, the use is recorded against no pack, the balance goes to −1, and a `class_pack_deficit` exception sends the office to sell the next pack, which absorbs the deficit. Adults hold a normal enrollment in a slot, so capacity and rosters are unchanged for instructors.

Everything staff have not yet decided is a rule row rather than a design: `adult_no_show_consumes` (default false, matching today) with `adult_notice_hours` for a future notify-by policy; `class_pack_expiry_months` (default null); `class_pack_discounts_apply` (default false — an adult may be a parent in a household with enrolled children, and nobody has said whether the sibling ladder or the first-responder discount reaches a pack). The repeat-no-show report is built regardless. Void of a partly used pack refunds the unused lessons at the snapshot per-lesson price through the payment ledger, management-only.

Import: there is nothing to replay. Each adult's `Adult_Credit` value, where present, becomes one opening `class_pack` of that size with a null price (unknowns stay unknown), and every adult enrollment lands on an import review list for the office to confirm the balance. Adult billing-month rows import like every other pre-created month — as history where the month has closed, discarded with the reason "class pack" where it has not.

*Addendum 2026-09-16 (data profile of the 2026-09-14 export):* `Adult_Credit` is blank on all 127,697 `Lesson_Schedules` rows, so no opening packs come from it; every adult enrollment imports with an unknown balance and sits on the review list until the office confirms it. The decision stands; only its import clause loses its input.

Considered and rejected: folding packs into the credit ledger (mixes purchase revenue into the credit liability report); blocking attendance on an empty pack (gates the roll, which rule and ADR-0001 forbid); hard-wiring the model to Adult (a clinic sold as a pack later would be a redesign instead of a row).

Two discrepancies go to staff in batch 2 (qa.md Q110): FileMaker's rate table prices Adult monthly, which contradicts "no monthly tuition at all"; and the schedule gives an Adult class five seats where staff said six. Both are rows either way.
