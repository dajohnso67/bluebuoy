# No authorization balance tracking for institutional payers

Status: accepted (2026-09-07 · wayfinder ticket [#4](https://github.com/dajohnso67/bluebuoy/issues/4))

Early planning modeled charter-school and Regional Center funding as an `authorization` — a lesson or dollar cap with an expiry, consumed by attendance and warned on when near its limit. The billing team's confirmed reality (context package 2.10) is that no such balance is visible to Blue Buoy: families obtain their own funding and purchase orders, Blue Buoy invoices against them at month end, and collections work by contacting families near month end when no PO has appeared. We decided **not** to track authorization balances at all — the absence is deliberate, not an oversight.

What replaces it: a **funding reference** per student per payer (a charter PO or a Regional Center contract; reference number, kind, optional validity range — granularity refines when the institutional billing artifacts land), where *absence for a billing period* drives action: a `billing_run_exception` and a daily coverage-view line, replacing the nightly cap-check cron, both authorization events, and the authorizations endpoint. Around it: an invoice AR lifecycle (`draft → submitted → partially_paid → paid`, submission method snapshotted), `payment_application` rows so one check settles several invoices, UCI numbers and coordinator contacts as structured fields on `payer_assignment` (billing/management access only), and staff-controlled sibling-tier assignment so the deliberate undiscounted-first-tier strategy for funded students stays possible.

Considered and rejected: tracking caps defensively anyway — it would model data nobody at Blue Buoy possesses, invent a maintenance burden staff explicitly don't carry today, and reproduce a corrected over-design the package records as a known error.

Also confirmed here: **QuickBooks stays the accounting system**; the app records invoices, payments, and check references and reconciles with it, never replaces it. The glossary term is **funding reference** (umbrella), with "PO" the staff-facing label for the charter kind — "purchase order" would be a false name for the nine Regional Center agencies.
